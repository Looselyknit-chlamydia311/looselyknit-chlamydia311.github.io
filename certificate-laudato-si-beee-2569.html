English summary

Theerawat Thungkham received a certificate recognizing his role as a member of the organizing committee for the Laudato Si’ environmental education leadership seminar and nature classroom / sustainable development workshop using BEEE, held 2–4 July 2026 at Saen Palm Training Home, organized by the Education and Social Departments of the Archdiocese of Bangkok.
